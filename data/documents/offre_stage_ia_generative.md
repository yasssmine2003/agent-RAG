large language models. Your work will include building a retrieval-augmented
generation (RAG) pipeline over internal documentation, designing tools for an
agent, and evaluating answer quality.

- Knowledge of NLP and transformer models
- Familiarity with Docker and Git
- Bonus: a personal project involving LLM APIs or vector databases
